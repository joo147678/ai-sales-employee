## 3. ICP

**Core rule:** a person who owns a real, proven business with real expertise, whose results and judgment are stronger than how the market sees them online, and who can afford the offer.

### Segment A has two types: A1 big media buyers, A2 young service solopreneurs (Mohamed, 2026-11-24)

#### A1: Media buyers / performance marketers (ICP since 2026-11-21)
Freelance media buyers, performance marketers, growth/scaling specialists, and media-buying agency founders.
- **Why they fit:** fast, large cash flow; they sell a high-trust service where the buyer (a brand owner) needs to trust their thinking before handing them the budget.
- **Who qualifies: the "big" media buyers only (Mohamed, 2026-11-21).** Both conditions must be met:
  1. **Results threshold:** documented results of **EGP 500,000+ in sales for a brand they work with** (500K up to 1M+ and above). Evidence = case studies, screenshots, highlights, bio or posts with the number. Below 500K → not this segment.
  2. **The content gap: at least one of these:**
     - They don't produce content properly (little or no content, or content that doesn't show their work).
     - Their results exist but sit in **Highlights** only; they don't talk about them in their content.
     - They do talk about results, but their content speaks to **beginners**: marketers or business owners who sell below that level, people just starting out, or beginner tips. (So their content attracts the wrong audience, not brands that can pay them.)
- **Not this segment:** media buyers under the 500K threshold; those whose main business is selling beginner courses (judge as a course business); pure employees with no client business; those whose content already speaks clearly to big brands with their results (no gap).
- **Economics:** the 500K+ results are the economics signal. Never assume income; budget is confirmed on the call.

#### A2: Service solopreneurs (Mohamed, 2026-11-24)
- **Who:** a solopreneur, roughly **18–27 years old**, who sells a **service** (selling courses is also OK). Examples: AI automation / AI implementation for businesses (helps companies put AI into their work), B2B sales services, and service providers in general.
- **Why they fit:** they need content to bring in more clients; their skill is real but the market doesn't see it yet.
- **Qualifies when:** they need content to bring them more clients (little content, generic tips, or content that doesn't show their work). Visible clients, offer or case studies are a plus, **not required** (Mohamed, 2026-11-24).
- **Course sellers are OK** (Mohamed, 2026-11-24).
- **Not this type:** anyone whose audience is students or beginners (they target learners, not businesses or buyers who can pay).
- **Offer fit (Mohamed, 2026-11-24):** between the 1:1 and the program/academy. Mohamed decides on the call. Never assume income; write what you saw (clients, prices, team).

### Segment B: Domain experts with a company (Mohamed, 2026-11-24)
- **Who:** experts in their field: consultants, coaches, accountants, tax consultants, lawyers, language instructors (English or any language), clinic owners; also B2B consulting/advisory, AI implementation, operations/strategy and career consulting.
- **Age:** roughly **30–45 years old**.
- **Company:** owns a company of about **11–50 employees**. Smaller than that is still a strong signal to work with them when the other signals are strong.
- **The gap:** their expertise is stronger than how they look online.
- **Economics:** Egypt offer of EGP 10K–20K+ = strong, ~5K = borderline; Gulf/Europe ≈ EGP 25K–30K+ equivalent.

3. **Consultation:** a paid session with Mohamed (today: the 1-hour strategy session).

## 6. Voice and message rules (summary; full rules in PLAYBOOK section 5)

- Natural Egyptian Arabic with original English business terms. Analytical, bold in argument, never loud, hype or academic. Gulf/English prospects: same tone in their language.
- First message: max ~80 words. Follow-ups: under 60 words. After the prospect's first reply: max 3 lines, or Mohamed sends a voice note.
- No template skeleton repeated across prospects (e.g. "لاحظت إنك… وده عجبني جدًا بس شايف إن فيه فرصة كبيرة…"). Mohamed's prospects compare notes and spot AI copy.
- One message = one insight (a diagnosis, not a tip) + one low-friction question. Ask permission before sending any asset ("تحب أبعتلك…؟").
- Assets: a teaser, never the full audit (full diagnosis happens on the call). Preferred assets: one ready hook or Reel script in their voice; a video of Mohamed's face under 90 seconds, sent only after they say yes.
- Check every link (open it) and send it once. Verify titles (أستاذ / دكتور) before using them.
- Mohamed sends every message and owns every conversation. No assistant introduces himself as a middleman.
