# Research: outreach that closes high-ticket services ($1,000+) — 2026-11-21
Caveat: most numbers come from video-tool vendors (Loom, Sendspark) and US cold email, not Egyptian DMs. Treat them as direction, not guarantees.

## Does video work?
Yes, with conditions:
- Personalized video lifts replies from ~4% to ~10–16% (sometimes 20%+) **only when it is truly specific** to the person (firstsales.io, prospeo.io).
- A generic Loom link performs like plain text (Sendspark).
- **Permission first:** ask "want me to send a short video?" before sending. One team reports a 78% reply rate with opt-in video (Vidyard). You only record for people who said yes (ListKit).
- Length: 60–90 s cold; 3–5 min absolute max (prospeo.io). Yassin's Looms were 3 min.
- Video is getting saturated; some got more replies asking permission to send a custom research piece (Skool lead-gen). The key is "made for you", not the format.
→ For Mohamed: his own face, after permission, under 2 minutes, about ONE thing.

## Strategies
1. **Sample of the actual work, not an audit.** In high-ticket, the first message's goal is to earn a conversation, not close (Woodpecker). "Free consultation" asks got 0 replies; free demos that produce something tangible worked (Indie Hackers). For personal branding: a hook or Reel script in their voice.
2. **Dream 100 / warm-up before the DM.** Small list (start with 12–20), ~14 days of engagement before pitching (Cleverly, Sproutworth). Cleverly closed 2 of 12 companies that didn't know them.
3. **Interview / podcast invite instead of a pitch.** You show up as a host and connector, not a seller. One team reports ~30% of guests → strategy session, ~30% close (single source). The interview itself is a sample of the service.
4. **Warm outreach first** (Hormozi's Core Four: warm outreach, content, cold outreach, ads): referrals from existing clients, partners, and people who already know him.
5. **DM rhythm:** triage → trust → soft close; high-ticket buyers buy from "receipts" (specific evidence), not price tags (DM Champ).
6. **Lead with the top offer and downsell on a no** (Membership.io / Victor Damasio).
7. **Positioning first:** outreach only works when positioning is specific enough to name the prospect's problem (Consulting Success).

## Proposed flow for Mohamed's system
Small hand-picked list → 1–2 weeks of warm-up by Mohamed → short first message by Mohamed ending in a permission ask → after a yes, a work sample (face video <2 min or ready script) → one question → call only after they engage → top offer, downsell on no → referrals and interview invites running alongside cold outreach.
