## Insights
1. The first message is not the problem: reply rate is a steady ~20%. More volume alone won't change revenue.
2. 100% of the loss is between reply and call, two weeks in a row. Everyone who got an audit/Loom went silent. The asset gives value with no reason to book now.
3. "Shall I send you the audit?" buys a polite yes, not intent ("تفضل", "okay", "تمام وريني"). Islam agreed to the Loom and never opened it.
4. The two biggest losses aren't in Yassin's reports, only in the chats: Ahmed Hussein asked the full price and got only a booking link; Mostafa Mansour asked "what can you add?" then asked to stop the AI copy-paste. Real answers in Mohamed's own voice are required.
5. Targeting drifted to media buyers: ~19 of 25 in week 2. They are the ones who reply (10/17 vs coaches 0/7 English template, course sellers 0/5), but Yassin never checked the EGP 500K+ threshold now in CONTEXT §3 Segment A, and Eslam Hamdon's objection shows some don't feel the content gap.
6. Data gaps block learning: 7 of 32 not logged, no follow-up dates, inconsistent statuses.
7. Mohamed's Instagram is part of the sale: prospects check the profile after the DM; thin TOFU content lowers trust.
8. Math with the new prices: 1:1 is EGP 40K/month, capacity 2 → 2 clients = 80K (above the 70K November target). At ~20% reply, 30% reply→call, 25% call→close, ~35 messages/week is enough. The gap is conversion, not volume.

## Yassin's own week-2 recommendations worth keeping

- One CTA per stage: first message = yes/no question; after yes = asset + ask opinion; after clear interest = 10-min call; after call = offer.
- Follow-up 2 = one free, usable idea (series title or a positioning question), never a bare reminder.
- Three real interest signals before inviting to a call: asks about execution, names a current problem, states a business goal.
- Log every follow-up with date and type (reminder / diagnostic question / free value / call invite) and the asset type (Audit vs Loom).
