## Style answers
- Emojis: "بستخدم ايموجيز خالص مع الرسايل" (meaning to confirm: a lot, or none).
- "حضرتك": not for people close to his age (e.g. media buyers ~23–27). Yes for anyone older.
- Never: formal فصحى (very rarely).
- First message: two lines, not long.
- Voice note vs text: writing is better.
- Gulf/foreign: foreigners in English, never Egyptian.
