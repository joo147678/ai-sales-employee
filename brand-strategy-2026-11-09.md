## Positioning
"I'm a Brand Strategist & Personal Brand Coach. I help founders build profitable personal brands and businesses grow through content-led strategies."
- Core expertise: Brand Strategy. Personal Brand Coaching is an application of it.
- Value thesis: REEL → REAL REVENUE. Content is a business tool; revenue also depends on offer, sales and customer experience. No Reel is promised to produce a sale.
- Desired perception: he understands the business behind content, diagnoses commercial and brand problems, and makes deliberate decisions about how brand and content contribute to growth.

## Scope of work (what he sells)
1. Brand & Content Strategy: positioning, messaging, content strategy, idea system, alignment with growth goals.
2. Founder-led Idea Extraction: ideas from the founder's work, story, frameworks, decisions and lessons.
3. Authentic Scripting in the founder's own voice.
4. Camera Coaching & Production Guidance.
5. Editing Direction (or editing when in scope).
6. Content-led Business Growth: offer, lead, follow-up and operational issues that affect content results.
These are capabilities, not a revenue guarantee; not every client needs every part.

## Audience
- Dream Follower: an entrepreneur who wants to grow through content.
- Core ICP: a service business owner who wants to turn a personal brand into clients.
- Founder offer audience: founders and service owners with a meaningful offer and expertise who want personal branding to contribute to client acquisition.
- Business strategy audience: businesses that need brand clarity and content-led growth (detailed non-service ICP not yet validated).
- Priority situations: strong expertise not visible in content; reliance on referrals; content producing too few or poorly matched inquiries; production without a clear business objective; previous execution partners that missed the business or the founder's authentic voice.
- Prioritize business/problem fit and intent over follower counts, age, or beginner content creators.

## Four Mother Ideas
I. Content is a business tool / goal before content ("What job is this content doing for your business?").
II. Content is distribution (strong services don't make themselves visible).
III. Don't become Google: show diagnosis, judgment, context, evidence; not just tips.
IV. Content ecosystem / dynamic funnel: pieces play different jobs.
Signature concepts: Content Job, Relevant Reach / ICP Density, Messaging Before Production, Google Trap, Perception Filter, Belief → Context, Content Depth, Founder ≠ Creator, Owned Distribution.

## Channels
Instagram = primary (discovery + main inbound DM channel). YouTube = primary (depth, trust, methodology, case studies). Facebook = secondary (reach). TikTok = secondary (discovery).
Funnel is non-linear; the next step for a suitable lead is a Discovery Call; a DM is not a sale. Outbound outreach and content-generated inbound are tracked separately.

## Voice
Analytical, bold in argument (not loud), natural, conversational. Egyptian Arabic mixed with original English business terms (storytelling, production, scaling, content strategy...). Direct and approachable; no academic tone or jargon overload.

## Boundaries
No random content, trend-chasing, beginner-only marketing education or generic motivation. Proof only when genuine; no invented proof.
