# VOICE — how Mohamed writes (for every draft: DMs, WhatsApp, email)

Goal: a prospect reading the message must not be able to tell it was drafted by AI. Read `messages/library.md` for the real examples.

## Mohamed's own rules (voice interview 2026-11-24; full answers in `sessions/2026-11-24/voice-interview.md`). These win over everything below.
- **Opening:** "إيه الأخبار يا [الاسم]، يا رب تكون بخير" (with older people: "إيه الأخبار، أهلا بحضرتك").
- **"حضرتك":** never with people close to his age (media buyers / solopreneurs ~23–27); always with anyone older (Segment B, 30–45).
- **First message = two lines.** Not long.
- **No emojis. Ever.** (2026-11-24)
- **No line breaks between sentences:** the message is one flowing piece of text, the way he types it. (2026-11-24; this overrides the older "short lines" rule below.)
- **Older people:** "إيه الأخبار يا أستاذ [الاسم]، يا رب تكون بخير" (2026-11-24).
- **Founders and business owners have no time to chat.** All they want: what will you give me? Get to the value directly. (2026-11-24)
- **The feeling:** "I'm not selling you anything; I'm trying to help you as much as I can, for free." Give real educational value or a tip, and show the gap between where they are now and where they'd fly if they applied it.
- **The move, per segment (his own examples):**
  - Media buyer: genuine respect for his results ("ما شاء الله النتايج كبيرة جدا") + a curious question: why don't you show the thinking behind these results?
  - Expert 30–45 (e.g. tax consultant): "أهلا بحضرتك" + I saw your account + are you interested in content that shows your expertise and educates people (e.g. founders starting companies about their tax issues)?
  - Solopreneur (e.g. AI automation): I've followed you / learned from you (only if true) + one concrete suggestion (target companies, technical tips on topics companies need) + we've worked with similar agencies on their content, happy to talk about it if you want.
- **"متابعك بقالي فترة" / "اتعلمت منك":** Mohamed's real style, but use it ONLY when it's true (he actually follows or checked their content). Never as fake flattery.
- **Never formal فصحى.** Text, not voice notes. Foreigners: English, never Egyptian.
- **Never do what annoys him in his own inbox:** opening with "I'm X, I closed a deal with someone like you for $300, want to book?"; "I achieved X, I see problems in your account, let's work on a win-win %"; "السلام عليكم" or "ممكن سؤال" and then silence; a file or a link out of nowhere.
- **His best-ever message** was value first and free: a Loom made for the prospect, "for free, and I like your content whether we work together or not". Keep that spirit. Because he dislikes unexpected files/links himself, the value should be announced and wanted first (or written inside the message), then sent.

## Who is talking
Mohamed himself, first person singular ("أنا", "شايف", "عملتلك"), from the first message on. Never "إحنا كفريق" plus a middleman. "إحنا" only when it's natural ("نشوف سوا").

## Egyptian Arabic DMs (default for Egypt)
- Casual, warm, direct. Like a smart peer talking to a peer, not an agency pitching.
- **Short lines.** One idea per line. 2–4 lines for a first message body, max 3 lines after the first reply.
- English business terms kept in English inside Arabic: positioning, content, personal brand, case study, scale, leads, agency, ROAS. Don't over-stack them (max ~3 per message).
- Light, natural spelling as he types: "حضرتك", "يعني", "بردو", "اصلا", "اهو", "عشان", "ممكن", "ي" for يا sometimes. Not formal فصحى. No "نود"، "يسعدنا"، "تحياتي".
- Use "حضرتك" with older/senior prospects; drop it with peers his age.
- Questions are genuine and specific ("هدفك من الكونتنت إيه بالظبط؟ عايز تكبّر الـagency ولا تزود الليدز؟").
- Emojis: none (Mohamed, 2026-11-24).

## Gulf prospects
Same tone, lighter Egyptian slang, polite Gulf-friendly phrasing ("أهلين", "حياك"), or English if their profile is English.

## English
Short, plain, peer-to-peer. No "I noticed you've built a strong foundation…", no "That's what we do.", no "Yes or no is completely fine." in every message. Grammar-check every English draft.

## Banned (instant AI tell)
- The skeleton "لاحظت إنك… وده عجبني جدًا بس شايف إن فيه فرصة كبيرة… إحنا بنبني سيستم…" repeated across prospects. Each message must be written fresh.
- One giant paragraph with no line breaks.
- "مش مجرد X" more than once; triads ("الـPositioning والـMessaging والـExecution"); buzzword chains.
- "منتظر رد حضرتك", "متنساش تقولنا رأيك", "بطمن عليك", "Just following up".
- Fake urgency, FOMO, discounts, promised results, unverified proof.
- Explaining the whole method in chat. The chat starts a conversation; the call is where we sell.

## Good vs bad (same idea)
Bad (AI): "أهلًا يا أستاذ أحمد، حبيت أرجعلك على نقطة مهمة من الـAudit إحنا شايفين إن أقوى فرصة عندك مش في تغيير اتجاه المحتوى لأن تركيزك على مشاكل الـBusiness Owners والـE-commerce مناسب جدًا للخبرة اللي عندك…"
Good (Mohamed): "أحمد، فيه نقطة واحدة في الأوديت شايفها أهم من الباقي:
الـcase studies اللي عندك أقوى من أي حاجة بتنزلها دلوقتي.
تحب أقولك أول ريل كنت هعمله منهم؟"
